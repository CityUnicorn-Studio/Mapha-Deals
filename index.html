🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At Mapha Deals, we respect your privacy and comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Because our app is designed to be lightweight and secure, our data practices are very simple:

  • What we collect: Only the information you voluntarily type into our forms (e.g., Name, Student Email, Campus, and Deal Requests).
  • How we store it: Your profile and request history are saved only on your own device using your browser's built-in LocalStorage. We do not have a central database, and we cannot see your data.
  • WhatsApp Integration: When you submit a deal request or a new listing, the app opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. This message is sent directly to our team via WhatsApp, which is governed by WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy.
  • Location Data: We do not track your GPS location. The map feature only shows deals based on the city or campus you manually select from the dropdown menu.
  • Cookies & Tracking: We do not use tracking cookies, third-party advertising, or Google Analytics.

Your Rights: You can delete all your saved data at any time by clearing your browser's site data or by clicking "Logout" in the Student Hub.

Contact Us: If you have any questions about your privacy, please contact us via WhatsApp at +27 69 844 0391.
